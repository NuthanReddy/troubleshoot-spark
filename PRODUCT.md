## Purpose

Help Spark readers and operators distinguish symptoms, follow the participants and their decisions, reproduce a case, and verify a resolution. The catalog covers the cases in the PySpark on YARN guides, not every exception Spark can emit.

## Constraints

The two Markdown guides remain authoritative in `reading-list/Data Engineering`. Publish their generated presentation on GitHub Pages. Follow the user's Protocol Explorer reference: a searchable catalog, interactive flow and synchronized explanation. Preserve source citations and distinguish illustrative traces from production telemetry. Every animated step must also be readable without motion.
